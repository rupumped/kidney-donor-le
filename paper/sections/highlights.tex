\begin{itemize}
	\item \textcolor{red}{Kidney donation costs the average donor about two and a half years of life expectancy (2.4~years in the base case).}
	\item \textcolor{red}{This cost is largest for the youngest donors. Black donors face a larger cost than White donors at young ages, but the difference disappears by age~55, and men and women differ little.}
	\item This estimate depends heavily on an unresolved question in the literature: whether donor all-cause mortality risk rises only after roughly a decade post-donation, or not at all. Depending on which published estimate is used, the result ranges from a loss of months to years.
	\item Priority transplant access for donors and their designated family members is worth under one day on average, \textcolor{red}{but roughly 100--430 days if kidney failure actually occurs, depending on age at onset.}
\end{itemize}
