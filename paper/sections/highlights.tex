\begin{itemize}
	\item Kidney donation costs the average donor about six weeks of life expectancy.
	\item This cost is three times larger for Black donors than White donors, and nearly three times larger at age~25 than at age~55.
	\item The biggest source of uncertainty is donor hazard ratio.
	\item Priority transplant access for donors and their designated family members is worth under one day on average, but 100--500 days if kidney failure actually occurs.
\end{itemize}
