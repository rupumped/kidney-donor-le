\begin{itemize}
	\item Kidney donation costs the average donor about two and a half years of life expectancy.
	\item This cost is larger for Black donors than White donors, men than women, and at age~25 than at age~55.
	\item This estimate depends heavily on an unresolved question in the literature: whether donor all-cause mortality risk rises only after roughly a decade post-donation, or not at all. Depending on which published estimate is used, the result ranges from a loss of months to years.
	\item Priority transplant access for donors and their designated family members is worth under one day on average, but 100--500 days if kidney failure actually occurs.
\end{itemize}
