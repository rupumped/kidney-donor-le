\begin{itemize}
	\item Kidney donation costs the average donor about five weeks of life expectancy.
	\item This cost is nearly three times larger for Black donors than White donors, and more than three times larger at age~25 than at age~55.
	\item The biggest source of uncertainty is donor hazard ratio.
	\item Priority transplant access for donors and their designated family members is worth almost nothing on average, but hundreds of days if kidney failure actually occurs.
\end{itemize}
